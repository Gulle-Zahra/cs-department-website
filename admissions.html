Admissions

Begin your journey in computer science. Find everything you need to know about applying for our undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Degree Programs Offered

Undergraduate

BS Computer Science

4-year program (8 semesters) covering core CS theory, software development, AI, networking, and project work. 130 credit hours total.

Undergraduate

BS Software Engineering

4-year program focused on software design, testing, DevOps, and large-scale system development. 132 credit hours total.

Postgraduate

MS Computer Science

2-year research or coursework-based master's program with specializations in AI, Security, and Data Science. 30 credit hours.

Postgraduate

PhD Computer Science

Research-intensive doctoral program for candidates with a strong academic background and research proposal. Minimum 3 years.

Admission Criteria

Program Minimum Qualification Minimum Marks Entry Test
BS Computer Science FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS (with Math & Physics or Math & CS) 60% aggregate NTS / COMSATS Entry Test
BS Software Engineering FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS (with Math) 60% aggregate NTS / COMSATS Entry Test
MS Computer Science BS CS / SE / IT or equivalent (16 years of education) CGPA 2.5 / 4.0 or 60% GAT General (50%) or GRE
PhD Computer Science MS CS or equivalent (18 years of education) CGPA 3.0 / 4.0 GAT Subject + Interview

How to Apply

01

Check Eligibility

Review the admission criteria for your desired program and ensure your documents meet the requirements.

02

Register Online

Visit the official COMSATS admissions portal at admissions.comsats.edu.pk and create an applicant account.

03

Submit Documents

Upload your Matric & FSc certificates, CNIC, passport photo, and entry test score card.

04

Pay Application Fee

Submit the application processing fee via bank challan or online payment. Fee is non-refundable.

05

Appear in Test

Appear for the COMSATS entry test or submit a valid NTS/GAT score as per program requirements.

06

Await Merit List

Merit lists are displayed on the university portal. Selected candidates receive an admission offer letter.
